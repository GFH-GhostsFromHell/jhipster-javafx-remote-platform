# Section 12 support calculator split

The desktop workbench now keeps cable strapping separate from raceway support calculations.

- NMD90 cable straps: 1.5 m maximum spacing and 300 mm maximum from each box/fitting for the straight-run worksheet (CEC 2024 Rule 12-560(1)).
- BX/AC90 armoured cable straps: 1.5 m maximum spacing; termination distance depends on connector trade size: 300 mm for <=35, 600 mm for 36-78, and 900 mm for >78 (CEC 2024 Rule 12-618(1)).
- EMT, rigid metal, rigid PVC, flexible metal, liquid-tight flexible conduit and ENT remain in a separate raceway-support calculator.

The calculator is a worksheet aid and still requires field verification of installation-specific exceptions.
