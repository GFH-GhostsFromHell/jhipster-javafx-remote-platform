# Electrical Safety Workspace

Native JavaFX safety module added without replacing the existing Workbench, Learn, Field, Projects, Career, News, or Profile areas.

Includes:
- PPE / arc-flash rating comparison using incident energy from an authorized label/study
- Ontario construction overhead-conductor minimum-distance calculator (O. Reg. 213/91 s.188)
- User-entered CSA/employer shock and arc-flash boundary checker
- Unsafe-condition / near-miss records with attached photos stored locally
- Rubber insulating glove inspection and dielectric-test tracking
- Electrical safety-equipment maintenance tracking
- Thunderstorm / lightning safety tracker for substation and utility work
- Native ECCC MSC GeoMet `Lightning_2.5km_Density` snapshot centered on user-entered site coordinates
- One-click official ECCC live Lightning Map
- IHSA Electrical Utility Safety Rules Rule 108 stand-down summary
- Hydro One public 30–30 thunderstorm guidance with a 30-minute stand-down timer that resets on new thunder/lightning observations
- Flash-to-thunder distance estimate and locally stored stand-down / restart-clearance history

The thunderstorm module is deliberately conservative. A clear map, elapsed timer, or saved clearance entry never authorizes work. The current employer/site procedure, EUSR Rule 108, work-protection requirements, detector alarms and responsible supervisor/work authority control stand-down and restart decisions.

The Safety workspace is a planning and recordkeeping aid and does not authorize energized work or replace employer hazard assessments, CSA Z462 procedures, manufacturer instructions, applicable legislation, or utility-specific work methods.
