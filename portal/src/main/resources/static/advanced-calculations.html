Neutral Sizing

Neutral current

For 3-phase line-to-neutral loads separated by 120°, vector neutral current = √(A²+B²+C²−AB−BC−CA). Apply only the demand reduction permitted by the applicable CEC rule.

Advanced Motor Feeder

Multiple motors, simultaneous starting, feeder ampacity and feeder OCPD arithmetic.

Multiple-motor feeder

Worksheet basis: feeder conductor = largest motor × factor + other motors + other loads + any simultaneous-start adjustment required by the applicable Rule 28-204 method. Feeder OCPD arithmetic uses the largest branch OCPD plus the remaining motor/load currents; confirm the exact CEC subrule for the installation.

Transformer Fault & Protection

Transformer / bank calculator

Approximate secondary terminal fault current assumes an infinite primary source: Isc ≈ FLA × 100/Z%. Actual available fault current also depends on upstream source and conductor impedance.
