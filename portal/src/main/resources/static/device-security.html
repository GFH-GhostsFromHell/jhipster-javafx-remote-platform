Recovery and owner controls

Important: Your ability to recover your computer must never depend exclusively on this portal. Use your operating system's supported recovery process, keep an independently accessible copy of the recovery key, and confirm backups before making any encryption changes.

This initial version intentionally excludes remote commands, automatic encryption, drive locking, key extraction and cloud escrow. Future managed recovery would require verified enrollment, owner approval, encrypted key storage and audited access.
