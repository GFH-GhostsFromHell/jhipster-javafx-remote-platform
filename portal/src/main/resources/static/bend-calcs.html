Conduit bending, explained visually.

Choose the bend you are making. Each calculator shows the geometry, field inputs, shrink or spacing, and the actual A/B/C/D marks on a straight conduit before you bend it.

Bender take-up/deduct, star marks and shoe radius can vary by tool. Confirm the values stamped on your bender before making final field bends.
Geometry is calculated from conduit centerline dimensions. Results are rounded for field layout; rounding can accumulate on multi-bend work. This workspace is a trade-math aid, not a substitute for the bender manufacturer's marks or job-specific layout checks.
