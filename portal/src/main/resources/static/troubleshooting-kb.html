Equipment Troubleshooting Guides

Structured methods and fault-isolation checklists for industrial electrical, controls, mining equipment, protection and commissioning. Includes Epiroc-style interview troubleshooting examples, but these are general training procedures, not Epiroc OEM service instructions.

Safety gate: Assess hazards and authorization first. Follow site procedures, lockout/tagout, isolation of all energy sources, and verification of absence of voltage with an appropriately rated tester. High-voltage, battery-electric, stored-energy, hazardous-area and underground equipment require OEM/site-approved procedures and suitably qualified people. Do not bypass safety circuits or protective interlocks. Troubleshooting steps are not authorization for energized work.

Universal 7-stage diagnosis

  1. Establish a safe work condition and identify all energy sources.
  2. Confirm the complaint and collect operator history and alarm logs.
  3. Read the OEM diagram, system sequence, and permissives.
  4. Split the problem into supply, control, output, and mechanical sections.
  5. Test systematically using approved equipment and compare with expected values.
  6. Correct the cause and function-test through the approved return-to-service procedure.
  7. Document readings, repairs, settings, and follow-up work.
